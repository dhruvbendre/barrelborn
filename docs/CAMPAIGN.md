# Campaign outline (working)

Fixed endpoints: **a kid floating in a barrel** → **Pirate King**. The barrel ends the game in the captain's cabin.

| # | Island | Local problem | New power (combat + exploration) | Crew / progress | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gullhaven** | Chainhook taxes the harbor and holds Sol | Gale Slash (ranged, switches, cracked stone, cancels shots) | First ship *The Brine Dream*, navigator Sol, first flag | **Built (blockout)** |
| 2 | Broken Lighthouse | The light is out; ships wreck on the reef | Grapple line (swing points, pull shields off enemies) | Shipwright-gunner joins; ship gets a cannon | Outline |
| 3 | Blockade Strait | A navy blockade seals the trade lanes | Reputation: wanted posters rise, NPCs react | Cook / quartermaster joins | Outline |
| 4 | Hidden Reef | A map that only shows at low tide | Tide Sense (reveal hidden paths, underwater pockets) | Discovery of the legendary route | Outline |
| 5 | Iron Keep | Crew captured; Kito alone | Storm Surge (Gale + Grapple combo burst) | Rescue the crew | Outline |
| 6 | The Last Horizon | The final passage and the rival who claims the crown | All powers | Pirate King recognition; the barrel in the cabin | Outline |

The ship is the hub and destination selector; later upgrades change its look. Crew are story/hub characters with occasional authored assists.
Out of scope: seamless ocean, naval sim, multiplayer, playable crew roster, crafting, loot treadmill, procedural main path.
